Our Mission

At SpeakNova English Academy, we believe that language should never be a barrier to your dreams. We were founded on the principle that mastering English opens doors to new careers, deeper connections, and global opportunities.

Unlike traditional classrooms that focus entirely on textbooks, we prioritize active speaking and real-world application. Our curriculum is designed to get you speaking confidently from day one.
